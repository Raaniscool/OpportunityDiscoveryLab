# Research sources and evidence quality

Accessed via web search on 8 November 2026; pricing is a time-sensitive snapshot, in USD unless specified. Vendor quotes are offers, not independently verified transactions. Customer discussions are anecdotes, not representative surveys. Internal links use these identifiers.

- [S1] [Plausible, paid analytics versus free GA](https://plausible.io/paid-analytics-vs-free-ga) — vendor comparison; $9/month starting price.
- [S2] [Better Stack pricing](https://betterstack.com/pricing) — free tier and $29 annual-billed responder/license price; check exact product scope.
- [S3] [GitHub Copilot plans](https://github.com/features/copilot/plans) — $10 Pro, $39 Pro+, credits; changing allowances.
- [S4] [Shopify revenue share FAQ](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/rev-share-faq) — eligibility, lifetime threshold since 2025, processing and registration. Another Shopify help page describes an annual threshold; the FAQ and [partner terms](https://www.shopify.com/partners/terms) say lifetime. Verify with Shopify before relying on it.
- [S5] [Stripe Connect pricing](https://stripe.com/connect/pricing) — processing and platform-dependent fee structure.
- [S6] [OpenAI GPT-4.1 mini model page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-4.1-mini) — token rates, model currently labeled deprecated. Illustrative only, confirm current models and pricing.
- [S7] [Steam-hosted article on game pricing](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=413150&enddate=1508942981) — developers discuss pricing, not a representative sales dataset.
- [S8] [Stripe Billing pricing](https://stripe.com/billing/pricing) — card rate; Billing may have additional fees.
- [S9] [Ask HN: bought a SaaS, declining](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=37162210) — owner reports installs and uninstalls; unverified, single business.
- [S10] [Everhour founder: pricing mistakes](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=45298374) — self-reported free/paid numbers and tier design experiences, not a cohort conversion rate.
- [S11] [TechCrunch: Bench shutdown](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/27/bench-shuts-down-leaving-thousands-of-businesses-without-access-to-accounting-and-tax-docs/) — reported shutdown and customer disruption.
- [S12] [TechCrunch: Bench acquired](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/30/bench-to-be-acquired-after-abruptly-shutting-down/) — revival; buyer reported approximately 12,000 customers, different from earlier marketing claims.
- [S13] [TechCrunch: inside Bench's fall](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/03/inside-the-wild-fall-and-last-minute-revival-of-bench-the-vc-backed-accounting-startup-that-imploded-over-the-holidays/) — reported staff accounts and reported debt pressure; causality uncertain.
- [S14] [Founder startup postmortem](https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/comments/1oks6qi/postmortem_on_spending_15_year_building_and/) — self-reported build, costs, shutdown; no independent verification.
- [S15] [Microsoft FY25 Q4 earnings call](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/events/fy-2025/earnings-fy-2025-q4) — company-reported Copilot adoption, not paid users or causal evidence.
- [S16] [Shopify App Store: RevSplit](https://apps.shopify.com/revsplit) — $19/month entry price; one listed app, not proof of significant revenue.

Search process: web searches for vendor pricing, public company earnings, founder/customer discussions, AI costs and shutdowns. Evidence deliberately spans successes, failure and adverse customer experience. No private customer data, interview study, purchase orders, actual acquisition experiments or verified market sizing were available. Prices and forecasts in opportunity documents without source tags are analyst assumptions, not researched sales figures.
