Build cost is hours × $75/hour fully loaded; not cash spend if founder self-builds, but a real opportunity cost. ## What would change the ranking?

Evidence is category-level, not proof of unmet demand for these exact wedges. Agency reporting ranks first because a definable workflow and partner route offer an inexpensive test; it still competes against free tools. Certificate tracking has clearer pain but costly sales and correctness exposure. The Shopify case has a marketplace route, but the $19/month listing [S16] and platform free offerings signal price pressure, not demand for our proposal. Maintenance needs a bounded contract, exclusion of unsupported stacks and explicit response times; it is not scalable at software margins. The converter should be narrowed to a real format and buyer group before any estimate is trusted. Rankings 6–20 are progressively more speculative; marketplace and ads face two-sided liquidity and traffic risk respectively.

## Discovery gates — no development authorized

1. Pick one narrowly defined workflow and interview 15 independently sourced target buyers, including at least five not referred by a friendly partner. Ask for recent examples, costs, existing spend, decision maker and alternatives; avoid leading questions.
2. Obtain at least three independently paid, refundable pilots at proposed price and document scope. For maintenance, validate on existing systems only with authorization; for others, use a manual mockup/service rather than building. A verbal commitment is not payment.
3. Record outreach hours, cash spend, conversion, delivered value and actual support cost for each pilot. Require two of three to renew or repeat purchase at full price after a real cycle, and a credible route to CAC payback under six months based on observed gross contribution. A small pilot is not statistically conclusive.
4. Stop or substantially narrow if buyers will not pay, onboarding depends on unsupported integrations, claims cannot be verified safely, or actual delivery margins cannot cover acquisition and founder compensation. Seek legal/security advice before handling regulated or sensitive documents.
