# Research Sources Registry

Research cutoff / access date: **2026-11-08** unless a different access date is stated. No source dated after 2026-11-08 is used. Inline citations in the other files use the search-result numeric id and URL; this registry groups sources by stable IDs (S01…) for auditing.

## Evidence grades

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A | Government/official statistics, regulation, or audited transaction data. |
| B | Large independent survey, peer-reviewed study, or primary vendor dataset with clear methodology. |
| C | Vendor survey/pricing page, small sample, secondary reporting, or cooperative contract listing. |
| D | Low-quality syndicated market report, anecdote, or unverified claim. |

Bias flags: **V** = vendor-sponsored or vendor-published; **S** = secondary reporting; **P** = pricing page snapshot; **F** = forecast, not actual.

## Citation conventions

- Inline citations use `[id](url)` from web-search results. IDs reset per query; the URL disambiguates.
- Market sizes always carry a source and reference year/release date.
- Vendor claims are labeled as vendor claims; forecasts are labeled as forecasts.
- Pricing is a snapshot unless a publication date is given.
