## Evidence rules applied

- CMS refresh dates are not episode dates. O&O #115 uses Apr 23 2024; #169 uses Feb 13 2025; PoolPro Great Valley uses Jan 5 2022.

- Vendor case studies (Quo, Exscape, Jobber comparison pages) are graded D and not treated as independent proof of willingness to pay.
- Job postings prove recurring workload and budget, not explicit dissatisfaction.
- No source dated after 2026-11-08 was used.
