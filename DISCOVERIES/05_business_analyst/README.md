# Agent 05 — commercial viability research

Research snapshot: 8 November 2026. Scope: independent commercial research only; no product development. USD unless noted. Prices and platform rules can change; verify before committing capital. Evidence of category spending is not evidence of demand for any proposed product.

## Decision

1. **Niche agency reporting / exception reconciliation** — most credible first test, *if* a reachable agency partner can introduce buyers; $149/month hypothesis, with manual setup and an explicit stop rule.
2. **Contractor certificate-of-insurance follow-up** — recurrent, costly workflow, but incumbent and liability risks are serious; $199/month hypothesis.
3. **Shopify product-feed exception monitor** — distribution exists, but marketplace discovery, free tools, platform dependence and churn are major risks; $49/month hypothesis.
4. **AI-generated app maintenance service** — paid pilot and recurring maintenance may be viable, but a service business is constrained by labor; do not mistake billings for SaaS margins.
5. **Professional desktop file-conversion tool** — strongest non-subscription alternative when a demonstrable recurring service is absent; distribution and compatibility determine viability.

These are *relative* rankings, not verified opportunities or earnings forecasts. The first step is paid discovery, not building. Evidence is mostly competitor pricing, public company statements and individual experiences; no prospective-customer interviews were conducted. None has verified demand, CAC, retention, or unit economics.
